# UK-Housing-Analysis

## Description
This project investigates UK house-price trends, regional differences and the relationship between house prices, earnings and GDP per head. 

## Data
The project combines various data from the Office for National Statistics:
- [UK House Price Index](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/uk-house-price-index-data-downloads-january-2026)
- [Median UK Earnings](https://nmweb.dur.ac.uk/query/construct/summary.asp?mode=construct&version=0&dataset=30)
- [Regional Gross Domestic Product](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/grossdomesticproductgdp/datasets/regionalgrossdomesticproductlocalauthorities?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- [UK Population Estimates](https://www.ons.gov.uk/file?uri=/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/populationestimates/datasets/populationestimatestimeseriesdataset/current/previous/v15/pop.csv)

## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
The EDA is split into two parts. The first part is a regional exploration of average house prices, median earnings and median local authority GDP per head from 2002 to 2023. The second part is an exploration of average house price and population across England (as a whole rather than the individual regions), Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland from 1971 to 2024. Both parts consist of summary statistics, data visualisations and time-series analysis. At the end of the EDA a price-to-earnings metric was derived, displaying the changes across each region from 2002 to 2023.
